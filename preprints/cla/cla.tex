\Abstract{
\pkg{cvxcla} is an open-source \proglang{Python} implementation of Markowitz's
Critical Line Algorithm (CLA), which returns the \emph{entire} mean--variance efficient
frontier as a finite list of turning points, exact up to floating-point round-off. The turning
points are Markowitz's; the package adds an interface to the covariance and a wider
range of constraints. The algorithm reaches the covariance only through a small
operator protocol, so a dense matrix, a diagonal-plus-low-rank risk model, a data
matrix, or a user-written backend can be exchanged without touching the loop. On a
synthetic market of 2560 assets and ten factors, where every asset is free at some
point of the frontier, the factor backend traces the same frontier about 30
times faster than the dense backend, and 5 to 6 times faster than a dense backend
that maintains the inverse of the free block by rank-one updates; on real data, where
few assets are free at once, the gain is smaller. Box bounds, linear equalities and inequalities, and a cap on
gross exposure are supported. The walk provably terminates under standard
nondegeneracy assumptions, every turning point is checked for feasibility as it is
found, every trace in this paper passes an independent Karush--Kuhn--Tucker
certificate, and inputs outside the supported domain stop with an
error that names the cause.
}
